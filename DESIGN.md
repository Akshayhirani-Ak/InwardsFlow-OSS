# InwardsFlow interface

A receiver reads the identifier, checks the quantity, then commits to a pallet. Keep those three elements together. Supervisors scan the registry by delivery state. Give each state a consistent place and show its count in the control that filters the registry.

## Type and dimensions
Poppins carries labels and prose. Set codes and compared figures in IBM Plex Mono with lining tabular figures. Use 32px semibold for counts, 24px for screen titles, 16–20px for section titles, 14px for body, 13px for secondary text, 12px for captions and 11px tracked capitals for section labels. Inputs on phones use 16px text.

Use 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40 and 48px spacing. Office cards have 20–24px padding and 16px corners. Controls have 12px corners. Dialogs have 20px corners. Badges and selected filters are pills. Use rest elevation on office cards, raised on hover, overlay on menus and dialog on modals. Keep list separators inside surfaces.

## Navigation and density
On office desktops use a 240px sidebar with role-filtered destinations and account actions at its foot. On office tablets use a horizontal icon rail. On phones put office destinations in a labelled bottom bar. The counting screen gives that space to the dock and opens navigation from its header menu. Preserve the mounted pad while viewing the phone manifest.

## Controls
Primary actions use white on royal blue. Secondary actions use a tinted surface. Show a keyboard focus outline, a pressed state and disabled feedback. Use an inline spinner for pending actions and written errors beside their control. Use 44px minimum interactive targets, 48px on floor surfaces. Enlarge the counting controls on tall tablets. Keep the commit dock outside scrolling regions.

## Verification
Check dense and empty lists, long identifiers and error states. At 1280×600 inspect eight complete manifest rows and the full counting cycle. At 390px switch to Products and back with a draft tally. At 800×1280 inspect the enlarged controls. Keep print geometry isolated from screen styles and honour reduced-motion preferences.
